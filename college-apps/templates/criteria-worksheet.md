# What are you looking for in a college?

Fill in whatever you can and leave the rest blank — blanks are useful information, they
tell us what to talk about. This becomes the starting point for your list, and you can
change any of it later. Nothing here is a commitment.

Takes about fifteen minutes. Do it with a parent for the money questions.
