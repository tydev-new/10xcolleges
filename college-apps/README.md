# College Applications — Essay Coach

A Claude plugin that coaches high school students through college application essays the way a great counselor would — uncovering authentic personal stories, breaking down prompts, and giving honest feedback draft by draft without ghostwriting.

Install from the [10xcolleges marketplace](https://github.com/tydev-new/10xcolleges).

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **student-intake** ✅ | Reads your school packet or chats with you to gather your background, activities, and reflections in your own words. Leaves blanks blank and never asks for sensitive financial info. |
| **essay-coach** ✅ | One essay at a time: builds a brief and rubric from college guidance, explores authentic angles from your life, and gives scored, draft-by-draft feedback — never ghostwritten. |

Full documentation is in the [repository README](https://github.com/tydev-new/10xcolleges#readme).
