### Template Structure

```markdown
# <College Name>

## Overview & Admissions
- **Admissions profile:** Admit rate <X>% [CDS YYYY-YY §C1], Middle 50% SAT/ACT: <Range> [CDS §C9], % submitting scores: <X>% [CDS §C9].
- **Major-specific selectivity:** Direct-admit status vs. pre-major weed-out pool for the student's intended major.
- **Admissions policy & leverage (CDS §C7):** Relative weight of GPA, rigor, essays, and recommendations; whether Demonstrated Interest is tracked; Early Action/Decision leverage.
- **Testing strategy:** Recommend submitting scores vs. test-optional based on student score vs. middle 50% and % submitting.

## Academics & Programs
- **Program & curriculum:** Exact degree name, accreditation (e.g. ABET), capstones, and co-op opportunities.
- **Essay hooks (for essay-coach):** At least 2 named, distinctive undergraduate resources (maker spaces, design project centers, specific labs, student-run co-ops) for "Why Us" supplemental essays.

## Costs & Financial Aid
- **Sticker vs. Net Price:** In-state vs. out-of-state tuition, total COA, and estimated net price [NPC YYYY-MM-DD / Scorecard].
- **Budget comparison:** Compare net price to the family's annual budget ceiling (from `criteria.md`), calculating the surplus or cost gap.
- **Merit & Aid policies:** Automatic matrix scholarships, out-of-state tuition waivers, or need-based threshold policies (e.g. full-tuition under $140k).

## Deadlines & Requirements
- **Application plans:** Early Action, Early Decision, Regular Decision dates [college admissions site].
- **Required supplements:** Essay prompts, portfolios, and priority deadlines for merit scholarships or FAFSA/CSS Profile.

## Fit & Friction
- **Watch out for (Friction):** At least 2 genuine, unvarnished watch-outs (secondary major gates, large lectures, harsh curve weed-outs, housing crunch, or weather).
- **Campus texture:** Qualitative student observations explicitly labeled `Impression, not data: ...`.
```
