### Structure & Conventions

```markdown
## University of Michigan — Target
- **Why it's here:** Strong mechanical engineering, match for research criteria [P1].
- **The numbers:** Admit rate 18% (CDS 2024-25), SAT middle 50%: 1350–1530.
- **The money:** Net price estimate ~$28,000/yr [NPC 2026-08-20].
- **Watch out for:** Highly competitive out-of-state pool.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-01 (EA) [umich.edu admissions].
```
