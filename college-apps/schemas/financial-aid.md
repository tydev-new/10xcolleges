### Key Requirements
1. **Strategy Archetype:** Must designate primary path (Need-Based, Merit-Seeking, or Hybrid/In-State Anchor) against the family's annual net price budget ceiling.
2. **Form Priority Dates:** FAFSA and CSS Profile deadlines must reflect institutional priority dates, which often precede admissions deadlines.
3. **Institutional Merit Grids:** Clearly distinguishes automatic, matrix-based tuition waivers from competitive scholarship applications.
4. **Local-First Scholarships:** Prioritizes high school bulletin and community foundation pooled awards over national lottery sweepstakes.
5. **Loan Separation:** Loans (Direct, Parent PLUS) are classified strictly as debt/financing and never subtracted to claim a lower net price.
