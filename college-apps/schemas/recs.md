## `brag-sheet--<teacher-slug>.md` — owned by rec-request (free-form body)

Context, specific projects, and memorable classroom moments the student shared with the teacher. To eliminate the generic "Kiss of Death" recommendation letter (damning with faint praise), every brag sheet must equip the teacher with **three concrete narrative bricks**:

1. **Coursework Context:** Course name, academic year, semester grade, and intended college major.
2. **Moment 1 (Intellectual Friction & Struggle):** A specific lab, problem set, or essay revision where the student experienced difficulty, sought help, and persevered.
3. **Moment 2 (Classroom Dialogue & Intellectual Risk):** A seminar discussion, debate, or question where the student took an intellectual risk or enriched peer understanding.
4. **Moment 3 (Independent Initiative / Culminating Project):** A term project, lab build, or paper where the student demonstrated self-directed curiosity beyond the syllabus.
