`conversations.md` is the append-only log of substantive student and family interactions. It captures verbatim phrasing, reflections, and insights that form the raw material for essays and recommendation letters.

## `conversations.md` — owned by student-intake (free-form body)

---

### Structure & Formatting

1. **Header Order:** One dated header per session, moving strictly forward in time (`## YYYY-MM-DD — <Session Topic>`).
2. **Verbatim Content:** Record what the student said in quotes, in their exact grammar and vocabulary.
3. **Attribution:** Every bullet point ends with its speaker tag (`[student]`, `[parent]`, `[counselor]`).
4. **Append-Only:** Never edit or delete existing entries. If a correction occurs, append a new bullet or section with today's date noting the change.

```markdown
# Conversations & Notes — Jordan K

## 2026-08-22 — Intake & background

- "I spent most of junior year fixing the robotics drivetrain because nobody else wanted to get their hands dirty." [student]
- "Biology maybe, idk — I like organisms more than chemistry." [student]
- "We haven't really talked about the college budget yet." [student]

## 2026-08-23 — Essay angle brainstorming

- "The bike stand outside the public library was actually my favorite project because random commuters would stop and talk." [student]
```
