**The prompt, plainly:** "What about Case Western attracts you?" — it is really asking:
show me you know what is distinct here, and that *you specifically* would use it.

**Word count and format:** 150 words maximum (admission.case.edu supplement page,
retrieved 2026-08-14).

**Rubric** — a strong response:

| Criterion | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Specific to Case Western | Names things that exist only there — think[box], a course, a professor's actual work. Not "great engineering." |
| Specific to Jordan | Connects to something Jordan has actually done, not aspirations |
| Two-way | Says what Jordan would contribute, not only receive |
| Fits 150 words | ~2 sentences of setup, tops |
| Not swappable | Paste another school's name in and it should fail |
