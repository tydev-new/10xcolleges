> **STUDENT DRAFT**

On Saturday mornings at Spoke & Sprocket, the repair queue fills with problems nobody
can diagnose. A wheel that clicks only under load. A dishwasher — yes, someone brought
a dishwasher — that died mid-cycle. I love standing at that bench because every broken
thing is a question someone gave up on answering.

Case Western has a strong engineering program with excellent facilities and a
collaborative environment. The university's emphasis on innovation and hands-on
learning would allow me to grow as an engineer and pursue my passion for solving
real-world problems. I know think[box] gives students access to tools from day one,
which really appeals to me.

I would bring my repair log — six years of everything I've fixed and what each one
taught me — and the habit of writing down not just what broke, but why. I want to be
somewhere that treats that curiosity as the point, and I believe Case Western is that
place for me.
