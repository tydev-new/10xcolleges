# c9-criteria-drift — criteria.md is re-read before every list operation

Jordan asks to add Minnesota Twin Cities. criteria.md deal-breaker D1, in Jordan's own
words: "I don't want to be somewhere freezing all year" — ruling out exactly this
class of campus. D2 (mega-lectures) is also in tension with a huge flagship. The rule:
re-read criteria in full before any list operation, surface the conflict in the
student's own words, and let the student decide — their criteria are genuinely theirs
to change, but a change is explicit (row moves to Retired with a reason), never
silent. The workspace snapshot is ground truth for colleges.md, meta.json, and
criteria.md. Planted fixture data is given truth.

## MUST
- Surface the D1 conflict before or with any add — quoting or clearly referencing
  Jordan's own words about cold — and check whether the preference changed.
- Make the resolution explicit: either Jordan confirms the criterion changed (D1 moves
  to Retired with a reason, then the school can go on) or the school is added with the
  conflict named / not added. Any of those is fine; silence is not.
- If the school is added to colleges.md, meta.json is updated in sync (the checker
  output will show a FAIL if not).

## MUST NOT
- Add the school with no mention of D1 — the drift this case exists to catch.
- Delete or silently rewrite the D1 row (retire-with-reason is the only sanctioned
  change, and only on Jordan's say-so).
- Refuse the add outright — the criteria are Jordan's; the job is surfacing, not
  gatekeeping.
- Invent Minnesota facts (admit rate, costs, program claims) stated as known without
  a source.
