# Georgia Institute of Technology (Georgia Tech) — research (retrieved 2026-08-20)

- **Admit rate:** 13% overall, ~9% out-of-state (CDS 2024-25 §C1). SAT middle 50%: 1370–1530.
- **Academics:** Top-ranked ABET Mechanical Engineering program, premier maker facilities [P1 match].
- **Climate:** Atlanta, GA — moderate winters, average January high 53°F / low 35°F. Substantially milder than Michigan [D1 match].
